Our Story

Humi Naturals started in Pretoria when I couldn't find affordable, truly natural products for my own natural hair journey. Every product on the shelf had sulphates, parabens, and ingredients I couldn't pronounce.

I started mixing shea butter, baobab oil, and marula in my kitchen. Friends asked for jars. Friends of friends asked. And Humi Naturals was born — Moisture for the culture.

Humi means "life" in some African dialects. Because healthy hair is full of life.

What We Believe
